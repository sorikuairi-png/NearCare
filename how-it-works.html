How It Works

From First Search To Confirmed Support

No swiping, no cold messaging, no guessing what happens next. Here's exactly what happens between describing what your family needs and having someone you trust in your home.

  1. 01

    Tell Us What You Need

    Describe the kind of support you're after — personal care, community access, capacity building, whatever fits — along with your postcode and funding type. Optional details like preferences or notes help sharpen the match, but nothing about the person you're supporting is ever named or shown.

    If there's no one near you yet: we'll tell you plainly rather than show you an empty page. NearCare is still growing area by area, and coverage keeps expanding.

  2. 02

    Browse A Ranked, Verified Shortlist

    You'll see independent support workers near you, ranked by fit — never hard-filtered out of view. Every result carries a fit indicator explaining exactly why it's ranked where it is, so it's never a black box.

    What "verified" means: every credential shown has been checked, not just self-reported, before a profile ever goes live.

  3. 03

    Message Safely, On Your Terms

    Sort out details through NearCare's in-app messaging before you ever hand over a phone number or email address. If either of you chooses to move the conversation elsewhere later, that's your call to make — not something the app does for you.

  4. 04

    Express Interest, Then Connect

    Found the right fit? Select Express Interest and the worker's notified directly — no cold messaging, no guesswork on either side. From there, you two sort out the details together.

    Recommended next step

    Loop In Your Support Coordinator

    Once you've agreed to move forward, it's worth contacting your own LAC or Support Coordinator — if you have one — so they can help formalise things on their end. NearCare doesn't hold that contact information and doesn't reach out on your behalf; this step is between you and them.

Common Questions

A Few Things Worth Knowing

What if there's no support worker near me yet?

We'll tell you plainly rather than show you an empty list. NearCare is still growing area by area, and coverage keeps expanding — it's worth checking back as more independent support workers join near you.

What exactly gets checked before a worker's profile goes live?

Every worker uploads their Working With Children Check, NDIS Worker Screening, and police check, plus proof of public liability insurance — and every credential is checked, not just self-reported, before a profile is visible in search.

Do I have to give out my phone number?

No. Everything starts through NearCare's in-app messaging. If you and a worker later choose to move the conversation elsewhere, that's entirely your call to make.

What if I don't have a Support Coordinator or LAC?

That's fine — that step simply doesn't apply to you. You and the worker sort out arrangements directly once you've connected.

Is this really free for families?

Yes. NearCare is free for families, guardians, and carers — always. Support workers pay a small subscription to stay listed; families never do.
